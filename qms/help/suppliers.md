## Suppliers

**Suppliers** lists every company whose product or service affects any of your devices, such as cloud hosting, AI model vendors, component suppliers and data partners. The list is shared by all devices; say in **Scope** which devices rely on each supplier.

New suppliers start as *Proposed* and can't be relied on until QA records a signed evaluation. Each supplier is due for re-evaluation after its interval (12 months by default) and appears on the dashboard when overdue.

## Evaluate a supplier

QA only.

1. Open the supplier and review the evidence: certificates, audit reports, questionnaires, contracts and past performance.
2. Under **Evaluate**, choose the outcome: *Approved*, *Conditionally approved* (state the conditions) or *Disqualified*.
3. Summarise the evidence and your conclusion in the notes, enter your password or signing PIN, and save. The next evaluation date is set from the interval.

## The SOUP list

The **SOUP list** (software of unknown provenance) records every third-party library, model or component in your devices' software: name, exact version, which devices and software items use it, purpose, licence and safety class.

At least monthly, normally during the SOUP review PMS activity, check each item for published anomalies and vulnerabilities, update **Known anomalies**, and set **Last anomaly review** to today. Items not reviewed for 35 days are flagged.
