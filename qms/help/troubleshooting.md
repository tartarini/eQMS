## Rules everyone follows

- **No patient or participant data**, anywhere in SAMD_QMS: no names, NHS or hospital numbers, identifiable images or screenshots, recordings or transcripts. Use anonymised site and study IDs.
- **Record it when you know it.** Log complaints on the day you hear of them; the date aware starts the reporting clock.
- **Never share your password or signing PIN.** Your signature means you personally approved something.
- **Nothing is deleted.** If a record was raised in error, reject it with a reason instead.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Access pending" after signing in | You have no role yet. Ask QA to add you. |
| ORCID sign-in says sign-up is closed | Sign in another way, then connect ORCID under Connected accounts. |
| Connected accounts has nothing to connect | Single sign-on isn't switched on for this site. Ask your administrator. |
| A step's button is missing | Read the red reasons under the step: fill in the field, add the link, or ask someone with the right role. |
| "Needs independent sign-off" | You wrote this record or version. Ask another QA member to approve it. |
| "Signature failed" | Re-enter your password, or your signing PIN if you set one; the PIN replaces your password for signing. |
| Can't set a signing PIN | Sign out and in again, then set it within 15 minutes. |
| "Choose one of the device's software items" | Pick an item from the suggestions. If the right one is missing, ask QA to add it on the device page. |
| "Locked against edits" | The record is closed. Raise a new record and link it. |
| "Integrity check failed" on a file | The stored file has changed since upload. Tell QA immediately; don't use the file. |
| No emails arriving | Check spam, then ask the administrator to check the email settings. |
| A device, record or document says "Not found" | It belongs to a device team you're not on. Ask QA on that team to add you if your work needs it. |
| "You are not on this device's team" | You can't act on that device's items. Ask QA on its team to add you. |
