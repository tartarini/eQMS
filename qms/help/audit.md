Everything is recorded: who created or changed what and when, with old and new values, every status change, signature, link and upload, plus logins, failed logins, role changes, device-team changes and connected sign-in accounts. Nothing can be edited or deleted, and each entry is chained to the one before it, so any tampering is detected. The dashboard and the **Audit** page show whether the chain is intact.

## Where to see it

Each record, document, hazard, supplier and device shows its own audit trail, to anyone who can see the item. The **Audit** page, for people with All-device access, shows everything and can be filtered to security events or signatures.

## Exporting

Use **Export all (CSV)** on the Audit page, or **Export CSV** under a record's audit trail, to hand the trail to an auditor. The export includes each entry's fingerprint so the auditor can check the chain.
