You're assigned training automatically when a document your role requires becomes effective, and you have a set number of days to complete it (14 by default).

1. Open **Training** in the top bar.
2. Click **Download to read** and read the document.
3. Enter your password or signing PIN and click **I have read and understood**.

You can only sign off your own training, and only for the current effective version. Overdue training appears on your dashboard and in your daily reminder email.

## For QA

- On a document's page, use **Require training** to choose which roles must be trained on it. People already in that role are assigned the current version straight away. For a device document, only that device's team is assigned it.
- New joiners are assigned their training automatically by the daily job.
- **Training → Training matrix** shows everyone against every document: completed, due, overdue or not required.
