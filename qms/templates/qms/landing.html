Quality management for software as a medical device

SAMD_QMS is the electronic quality management system used to develop, release and monitor software as a medical device (SaMD). It supports work to ISO 13485, IEC 62304, ISO 14971 and the UK and EU Medical Device Regulations. One installation serves many devices, each with its own team, records, risk file and technical documentation.

Request an account Sign in

Quality records

Problem reports, complaints, incidents, CAPAs, change requests and post-market surveillance, each following an enforced workflow with reporting deadlines.

Documents and training

Numbered, versioned procedures approved by electronic signature. Staff are assigned the training each new version needs.

Risk management

A hazard register scored before and after controls, with each control traced to the change that implements it.

Suppliers and SOUP

Approved suppliers and third-party software, each with its own review dates.

Technical file

Builds a device's technical documentation from live quality data, checked for gaps and approved by QA.

Trust

Approvals are signed, every change is kept in a tamper-evident audit trail, and people see only the devices they work on.

Who it is for

Engineers, quality assurance staff, clinical safety officers and auditors working on the devices hosted here. It holds no patient data.

Getting access

Accounts are for named people on a device team. Request an account by signing in with Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn or ORCID. QA reviews every request before you can see anything.
